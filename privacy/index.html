Privacy

flyingcar.pet has no accounts, no forms and no cookies. The simulation, its 3D models, fonts and footage all come from this site.

Server logs

The server keeps standard access logs (such as your IP address and browser type) to prevent abuse. They are deleted after 30 days and not shared.

WebMCP

The page's WebMCP tools run in your browser; nothing they do is sent to this site. If you connect an AI agent to them, what that agent reads is handled by the agent and its provider.
